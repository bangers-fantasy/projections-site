How to read this card

Scored against the best skater still on the waiver wire, on an 82-game basis. Ideal for H2H 12-team banger category leagues, 16 roster spots. The 3 scores at the top are that same score, split into offense and peripherals, which add to the total. They match the rankings exactly. The bar fill and the radar axes are percentiles, both among the top 350 forwards by banger rank; the number beside each category bar is the projected count. The radar’s dashed ring is the median. The quadrant plots both halves of the score in points. The crosshair marks the top quarter of each, and the shaded band runs from the median up to it. A corner name needs both halves clear of that band, so nobody gets called all scoring and no grit on median grit.
